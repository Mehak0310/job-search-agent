## Review lifecycle follow-up (2026-10-18)

Applied jobs must not return to active browser batches, packet queues or recommendations. Use both current/manual submission history and browser-reported/confirmed states; match stable employer/requisition aliases. Later interview/outcome status does not reset eligibility. Historical rows and immutable packets remain accessible; a browser-history toggle displays removed/prepared records without inferring submission evidence.

Missing/malformed links and confirmed closed/inactive vacancies are omitted from active review. User-reported unavailable links are persisted as dedicated metadata evidence and suppressed across source aliases, without deleting the underlying job. Do not infer permanent closure from CAPTCHA,403, timeout or temporary network failure. Cross-origin tab errors cannot be observed by the dashboard; provide a visible user report action.
