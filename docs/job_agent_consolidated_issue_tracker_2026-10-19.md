## Critical operating rules

No automatic final application submission, no Gmail send, no unapproved uploads, commits, pushes, deployments, production restarts or schedule activation. Real Gmail draft creation is also currently disabled pending a separate release decision. Never bypass employer-size, payroll, India eligibility or mandatory requirement gates. One active batch, max two workers, up to five eligible jobs. Do not erase historical submitted, unavailable or manual status evidence. Keep private candidate facts and real operational screenshots out of public Git. Mark historical test reports separately from observed live success. The focused tests in this revision used synthetic fixtures only; they establish the current gate and frontend behavior, not live performance, live Gmail, or employer form correctness.
